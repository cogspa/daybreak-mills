# Actual Studio features
Workspace starts from a brief/project. Projects supports saved projects, duplicates, checkpoints and recovery. Autosave happens locally. Front/Back/Left/Right/Top/Bottom buttons select panels. The box stays visible; click its faces or drag to orbit. Select layers to position, hide or lock; numeric fields are millimetres. Undo/Redo supports edits.
Full dieline: Workspace > Full dieline opens all six faces with optional guides; click a face to edit. This original six-face net is not a cutting die.
Sketch/import flattened art: Front > Brand logo > Edit asset, then slot Dieline art · size. Brush, Eraser, Fill and Mirror support FPO sketches. Import a square PNG made on Export > Download the dieline kit. Keep the full square, same size, hide guides; do not crop or reposition. Replace or overlay modes exist. Arbitrary printer PDFs/SVG dies cannot be imported and automatically mapped.
Folding carton: Workspace > Folding carton is a glued seal-end structural draft. Select size, Show artwork, Download draft SVG. Side glue flap, dust flaps, closure flaps; separate cut/crease annotations and suggested glue regions. Body artwork is mapped; top/bottom artwork is fitted to outer closures. It is RGB, lacks press bleed/board-thickness allowances, needs structural/closure approval. Not a certified die or folding simulation.
Packaging copy: Back and Left hold editable stories. Right holds ingredients, allergens and nutrition values. Select shared content or per-flavour overrides. Explicit blank overrides remain blank. Missing fields/overflow indicators are design checks, not regulatory certification. Decorative barcodes are invalid for retail use. Never invent nutritional values for a recipe.
Export: select products (this box, every flavour at this size, full matrix), review checks and tick Export as a design proof with listed issues. Tick Open Blender with the result, then Deploy to Blender. Pair through Connect to Blender (approve matching code locally). Render queue has progress, cancellation, retry, downloads and restore design version. Blender opens after building/rendering finishes. Exported references link to checkpoints. Current PDF/SVG are RGB proofs; CMYK is not installed. Explain printer ICC, bleed, cut/crease separation and approval as requirements to establish with the printer, not completed features.
Shelf layouts: Export > Blender scene > Layout. Studio lineup, vertical flavour blocks, one product per shelf, mixed assortment. 1–5 shelves, 1–12 facings, 1–3 boxes deep. Choose every flavour/full matrix for assortments. ZIP/deploy carries settings to Blender: shelf geometry, shared cartons, neutral lights and camera. The browser only previews cartons; shelf scenes appear in Blender. Each export produces one preset. No arbitrary per-slot drag-drop merchandising editor or full store aisle exists.
Ads: Ads tab selects flavour, poses, aspect ratios, background and slogan. Generate/export compositions for comparison. This does not publish campaigns or verify claims. Start by asking target audience, format/channel, message, substantiated benefits and campaign objective.
